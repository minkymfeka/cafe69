Food Parcels

This festive season, Café69 proudly partnered with Makro to give back to our community by delivering food parcels to families in need. As a brand built on community, gratitude, and shared success, this initiative reflects our commitment to making a meaningful difference beyond our doors.

We extend our sincere thanks to Makro for standing with us and helping spread hope during the festive period. To our community, partners, and supporters — thank you for being part of the Café69 journey. We wish everyone a warm, joyful, and blessed Christmas.
