Our Founder

Café69 was founded by Mr Mantambo, a visionary entrepreneur with a passion for excellence, community, and elevated social experiences. Driven by a clear vision to create a premium destination in Kwa-Thema, he built Café69 as a space where quality dining, refined entertainment, and sophisticated atmosphere could thrive under one roof.

For over a decade, his leadership has shaped Café69 into a trusted and respected brand — known not only for luxury and consistency, but also for meaningful community investment. His commitment to growth, innovation, and people continues to define the Café69 standard and legacy.

Meet The Team

Behind Café69 is a dedicated team driven by passion, excellence, and pride in what we do. From leadership to front-of-house and behind the scenes, each member plays a vital role in creating the refined dining, vibrant nightlife, and exceptional service our guests have come to expect.

United by a shared commitment to quality and community, our team brings the Café69 experience to life every day — delivering warm hospitality, attention to detail, and unforgettable moments that define our brand.

Community Projects

At Café69, community is at the heart of everything we do. Beyond being a luxury dining and nightlife destination, we are committed to creating positive impact through meaningful community initiatives that uplift, support, and inspire those around us.

Through strategic partnerships, sponsorships, and outreach programmes, we invest in local talent, provide support where it is needed most, and contribute to sustainable community development. Our community projects reflect our belief that true success is measured not only by excellence in business, but by the difference we make together.

The Café69 Experience

More than a restaurant or pub, Café69 is a social destination. It is where great food meets premium drinks, music sets the mood, and moments are created. Whether it’s an intimate dinner or a high-energy night out, every visit is designed to feel elevated and effortless.

Our Philosophy

Vision

At Café69, we believe luxury is not only about appearance, it is about consistency, atmosphere, and how people feel when they leave. Every detail, from our menu to our music and service, is intentionally curated to create an experience that feels refined, welcoming, and unforgettable.

10 Years of Excellence

For over a decade, Café69 has remained a trusted name in Kwa-Thema. This milestone reflects resilience, growth, and a deep connection with our community — built through quality, innovation, and an unwavering commitment to excellence.

What Sets Us Apart

From our luxurious interior and curated entertainment to our premium beverage selection and personalised service, Café69 stands apart as a destination that delivers both sophistication and soul.
